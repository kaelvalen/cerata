\title{Cerata: Transactional Memory for\\ Frozen Language Models}
\author{Draft --- \today}

\begin{abstract}
Personalizing a frozen base model should be a \emph{transaction}, not an edit:
every write gated, every fact attributable, every fact exactly revocable, with the
base untouched. We build such a store --- per-fact deltas behind an entity-gated
router, committed through a capability gate --- and measure each guarantee at
$N{=}1000$ on CounterFact and zsRE: efficacy $0.999/0.996$, distractor no-leak
$0.992/0.975$, exact revoke (retain $0.9989/0.9944$), leave-one-out provenance
$1.00$, and a canary footprint of $0.02$ KLD. Strong RAG reaches $0.837$ where we
reach $0.999$; the official GRACE/WISE implementations offer no forget or audit and
degrade or leak under the same protocol. We report failures and format
sensitivities in full.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
model editing, machine unlearning, retrieval-augmented generation,

\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}

Post-deployment learning is currently done by \emph{editing}: methods such as
ROME~\cite{meng2022rome}, MEMIT~\cite{meng2023memit},
GRACE~\cite{hartvigsen2023grace}, WISE~\cite{wang2024wise} and
AlphaEdit~\cite{fang2025alphaedit} write new facts into a model's weights. Editing
optimizes the \emph{write} --- how well a fact is learned --- but offers no way to
take a fact back, no way to attribute a served answer to a stored fact, and no
guarantee that the base's capabilities survive the write. Retrieval-augmented
generation~\cite{lewis2020rag} keeps the model frozen but puts knowledge in the
context window: personalization does not persist in the model and serving cost
grows with the store.

We argue that personalization of a frozen base should be a \emph{transaction}. A
store that holds facts must support three operations with operational guarantees:
\textbf{write} (a commit that is gated so the base's capabilities are preserved),
\textbf{forget} (an exact revoke that leaves the rest of the store untouched), and
\textbf{audit} (attribution of every served answer to the stored fact that caused
it). We build such a store --- Cerata --- and measure each guarantee at
$N{=}1000$ on two external benchmarks.

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{C1: Architecture and API.} A frozen base with a slot store of
per-fact LoRA deltas behind an entity-gated router; a transaction API
(\textsf{write}, \textsf{forget}, \textsf{audit}) with atomic commits, exact
revoke, and hash-based state audit.
\item \textbf{C2: Measured guarantees.} Operational definitions of efficacy,
no-leak, abstention, revoke (gone/return/retain), provenance, and canary KLD ---
each measured at $N{=}1000$ with bootstrap CIs: efficacy $0.999/0.996$, retain
$0.9989/0.9944$, provenance $1.00$, canary KLD mean $0.02$.
\item \textbf{C3: Scale and capacity.} One grouped delta carries 256 facts at
full efficacy with a canary footprint of $0.007$ KLD; per-fact cost is
$8.7$\,MB, revoke latency is $1.4$\,ms.
\item \textbf{C4: Honest baseline analysis.} Strong RAG reaches $0.837$ where we
reach $0.999$ at $N{=}1000$; the official GRACE/WISE implementations are
write-only and their numbers are serving-format dependent (GRACE: raw $1.00$,
chat $0.025$; WISE: per-step $1.00$, final $0.07$; with its merge lifecycle
$0.125$ and no-leak $0.27$).
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Evidence discipline.} Every run reported here was pre-registered
and pinned before execution; failures are reported, not patched silently
(Section~\ref{sec:results} lists an optimization instability, a data-slice bug
and its correction, and an RNG-position sensitivity). We consider this discipline
part of the contribution.

\section{Related Work and Positioning}

\noindent\textbf{Model editing.} Locate-then-edit methods modify MLP weights
(ROME~\cite{meng2022rome}, MEMIT~\cite{meng2023memit}); lifelong methods add
adaptors or side memories (GRACE~\cite{hartvigsen2023grace},
WISE~\cite{wang2024wise}); null-space constraints reduce collateral damage
(AlphaEdit~\cite{fang2025alphaedit}). The evaluation target is the write: edit
success, paraphrase generalization, locality. None of these systems exposes a
revoke operation, an attribution record, or a capability gate; sequential decay
over long edit streams is a known open problem.

\noindent\textbf{Unlearning.} Machine unlearning removes influence from trained
weights approximately and at coarse granularity. In our setting the unit is a
single fact and removal is exact at the store level: freeing a slot provably
removes the fact's parameters, and retention is measured on the rest.

\noindent\textbf{Retrieval.} RAG~\cite{lewis2020rag} keeps knowledge outside the
model. Its forgetting story is index deletion, but the knowledge never becomes
parametric: efficacy depends on prompting and context budget. Our strong-RAG gate
(Section~\ref{sec:results}) measures this gap at $N{=}1000$.

\noindent\textbf{Adapters and mixture-of-experts serving.} Per-task adapters and
MoE routing share our storage concerns; we add a per-fact lifecycle with
transactions on top of a frozen base. Table~\ref{tab:positioning} summarizes the
positioning.

\section{Architecture}

\noindent\textbf{Frozen base and slot store.} The base language model is never
trained. Facts live in \emph{slots}: each fact $i$ owns a rank-$r$ LoRA delta
$\delta_i$ trained on a fresh copy of the base. \textsf{materialize} writes the
slots into a shared LoRA bank; $\textsf{forget}(i)$ frees slot $i$ and
re-materializes, so the fact's parameters are exactly removed while every other
slot is bit-identical.

\noindent\textbf{Router.} A query is routed by an entity gate (normalized
substring match, longest wins; else fuzzy token match with edit distance $\le 1$,
$\le 2$ for tokens of length $\ge 6$) followed by a semantic key (a MiniLM
embedding of the probe, cosine similarity, threshold $\tau$). No candidate, or
similarity below $\tau$, yields \emph{abstain}: the base answers and the store is
not consulted.

\noindent\textbf{Serving.} If the gate fires, the probe is served by its own
expert; paraphrases are served by the routed fact's expert. Distractors that do
not match any entity abstain.

\noindent\textbf{Transactions.} \textsf{write} proposes a delta, computes the
canary KLD against the base, and commits only if it is below a cap ($2.0$);
a failed proposal leaves the store bit-identical (atomicity).
\textsf{forget} is exact revoke. \textsf{audit} exposes a SHA-256 state hash over
all deltas, per-commit records (fact id, KLD, seconds), and leave-one-out
attribution (Section~\ref{sec:guarantees}). Figure~\ref{fig:arch} shows the
system.

\section{Guarantee Definitions}
\label{sec:guarantees}

All guarantees are operational and falsifiable. Let $y_i$ be the greedy response
to a prompt for fact $i$ and $a_i$ its answer; \emph{contains} is token
membership.

\noindent\textbf{Serving.} \emph{Efficacy} $E$ is the fraction of probes with
$\text{contains}(y_i,a_i)$; \emph{paraphrase} $P$ the same on rephrased probes;
\emph{abstention} $A$ the fraction of distractor prompts that do not route;
\emph{no-leak} $L$ the fraction of distractors where the store introduces no
canary token that the base's own response did not contain (base-referenced).

\noindent\textbf{Revoke.} After revoking a sample of facts:
\emph{gone} $G$ is the fraction whose answer token is absent;
\emph{return-match} $R$ is the fraction whose post-revoke hit status equals the
pre-training base status (the base-referenced reading when the base already knows
answers); \emph{retain} $T$ is the fraction of kept facts still answered.
Token-collision and base-knowledge confounds are reported with the numbers.

\noindent\textbf{Audit.} \emph{Provenance} is leave-one-out attribution: an
answer is attributed to fact $i$ if it is present with $\delta_i$ active and
absent with $\delta_i$ suspended; the store state hash must return to its value
after the sweep.

\noindent\textbf{Capability.} \emph{Canary KLD} is the KL divergence between
base and store logits on canary prompts; the commit gate enforces a cap of $2.0$;
we report mean and maximum over sampled deltas.

\section{Experimental Setup}

\noindent\textbf{Base and data.} Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (28 layers, frozen).
Facts: a synthetic nonce set (seed $=n$) and the external CounterFact and zsRE
sets, first $N{=}1000$ facts each. Serving uses the chat template, greedy
decoding, and the contains rule; CIs are bootstrap ($2000$ resamples).

\noindent\textbf{Baselines.} Strong RAG: the fact's context is placed in the
system prompt with an instruction to follow it, at the same $N$. Official GRACE
and WISE: the EasyEdit implementations with hparams adapted to the 1.5B model
(28 layers; GRACE layer 18, $n_{\text{iter}}{=}50$; WISE layer 23,
$n_{\text{iter}}{=}70$, retrieve; fp16 for the shared 8\,GB GPU), sequential
edits, $N{=}200$; both the native (raw) and our chat protocol are reported.

\noindent\textbf{Pins.} All runs are pre-registered; the internal pre-registration
record (pinned before each run, SS33--SS63), raw logs and result JSONs accompany
the artifact.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:guarantees} is the frozen summary. \emph{Serving}: efficacy
$0.999$ (CounterFact) and $0.996$ (zsRE); routed paraphrase $0.323$ and $0.973$;
no-leak $0.992$ and $0.975$; abstention $0.992$ and $0.967$. The CounterFact
paraphrase gap is router-bound, not a delta limitation: with the delta served
directly, paraphrase reaches $0.86$--$0.90$ (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
zsRE paraphrases keep the subject, so routing holds and paraphrase reaches
$0.973$.

\emph{Revoke} ($n{=}100$): CounterFact gone $0.96$, return-match $0.96$, retain
$0.9989$; zsRE gone $0.36$ (base-confounded: the base often knows the answer, so
the token is present after revoke regardless), return-match $0.80$, retain
$0.9944$. Revoke latency is $1.4$\,ms; the store hash is stable across the
provenance sweep and provenance attribution is $1.00$ ($100/100$).

\emph{Capability}: canary KLD mean $0.0215$ (CounterFact) and $0.0171$ (zsRE),
maximum $0.0498/0.0529$ over $50$ sampled deltas --- two orders of magnitude
below the commit cap.

\noindent\textbf{Strong-RAG gate.} With an instruction-tuned context prompt at
$N{=}1000$, RAG reaches $0.837$ $[0.815,0.86]$ where the store reaches $0.999$
$[0.997,1.0]$: the store's advantage is durable parametric memory, not prompting.

\noindent\textbf{Capacity.} One grouped delta carries 256 facts at full efficacy
with KLD $0.007$ (Table~\ref{tab:capacity}). Genuine leakage onto held-out facts
is $0.1$--$0.3$ (base hit rate $0.0$), i.e.\ grouped deltas trade some exactness
for a $256\times$ storage cut; per-fact deltas are the exact configuration.

\noindent\textbf{Delta recipes.} On the nonce set, the baseline recipe scores
efficacy $1.00$, paraphrase $0.86$, KLD $0.036$; LoRA+ (B learning rate
$\times 8$) scores $1.00$, $0.90$, $0.014$ at equal size and is adopted for
per-fact deltas. OLoRA and DoRA break the capability cap (KLD $22$--$28$); MLP
targets give no gain at $2$--$3\times$ size; PiSSA mutates the shared frozen base
and is rejected. For grouped training, LoRA+ is unstable (see below) and the
baseline recipe is used.

\noindent\textbf{Failures (reported, not patched silently).} (i)~LoRA+ at
$8\times$ B-lr is unstable for grouped deltas: non-monotonic efficacy over
$k\in\{64,128,256\}$ because the grouped recipe performs $16\times 2k$ individual
optimizer steps --- a $64\times$ amplification of the per-fact case; it is
therefore per-fact-only. (ii)~The first capacity leakage check sliced held-out
facts inside the training group for $k>64$, reading leakage $1.00$; after the
fix (per-$k$ outside slice, base-referenced), genuine leakage is $0.1$--$0.3$.
(iii)~Training is deterministic given the RNG sequence; a $k{=}256$-only run
consumed a different draw position and scored efficacy $0.707$ --- recorded as
RNG-position sensitivity, not a capacity effect.

\section{Baselines: Format Matters}

Table~\ref{tab:baselines} places the baselines next to ours. \emph{GRACE}
(official) reaches $1.00$ raw-prompt efficacy ($200/200$ edits) --- in its
intended format --- but $0.025$ under chat-templated serving, because its keys
are built on the raw prompt's last-token activation; EasyEdit's own token-EM
metric reads $0.0$ for all targets due to a leading-space token mismatch (an
artifact we diagnosed). \emph{WISE} reports per-step rewrite accuracy $1.00$,
but the final model answers only $0.07$ of edits and leaks on $36\%$ of
distractors; with its intended merge lifecycle triggered within 200 edits, the
final model improves to $0.125$ while no-leak collapses to $0.27$ ---
consolidation trades per-edit accuracy for locality. \emph{RAG} reaches $0.837$.
No baseline offers forget or audit; the transaction gap is the contribution.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\noindent\textbf{Router-bound paraphrase.} CounterFact paraphrase (routed) is
$0.323$: rephrasings that drop the subject do not pass the entity gate. The
delta itself generalizes ($0.86$--$0.90$ direct), so this is an engineering gap
with a pinned fix (paraphrase-aware keys), not an architectural limit.

\noindent\textbf{Multi-fact subjects.} On zsRE the semantic pick among sibling
facts of the same subject is $0.784$, and revoke return-match is $0.80$ (sibling
interference). \noindent\textbf{Grouped exactness.} Grouped deltas leak
$0.1$--$0.3$ onto held-out subjects; exactness is a per-fact property.
\noindent\textbf{Scope.} One base model, one environment, no external
replication; the VLM mirror and the repair controller are validated at small
scale only (a $2$B VLM, an $18$-fact controller). \noindent\textbf{Formats.}
Baseline comparisons are serving-format sensitive; we report both formats and
treat the transaction guarantees --- not the editing numbers --- as the claim.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

Cerata makes personalization of a frozen base transactional: gated writes,
exact revoke, and auditable attribution, measured at $N{=}1000$ with tight
confidence intervals on two external sets. Editing baselines optimize the write;
none of them can forget or audit, and their numbers depend on the serving
format. Future work (paper~2): a learned router to close the paraphrase and
sibling gaps, format-matched baseline comparisons at equal $N$, grouped experts
with a coarse router for storage, and confirmatory-scale VLM and controller
evidence.
